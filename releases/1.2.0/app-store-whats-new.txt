KaamKoRecord 1.2.0 makes your item search more flexible and your everyday tools easier to reach.

YOUR OWN ITEM CATALOGUES
Upload a CSV and choose which headings to use for the item title, description, code and search. Use PLU codes for butcher items, or your own headings for groceries and other lists.

KEEP YOUR LISTS SEPARATE
Give each upload a name, such as Meat or Groceries. Your catalogues are private to your account. Choose a catalogue when searching, and update one list without replacing your others.

A REFRESHED DESIGN
Enjoy a clearer layout, a realistic analog clock and native mobile navigation. Home, Items, Work, Alerts and Profile put your main tools within reach. Clock, timesheets, pay and work settings are grouped under Work.

NOTIFICATIONS IN THE APP
Find notifications in the Alerts tab with an unread badge. Tapping a notification sent to the mobile app opens the relevant app screen, with Alerts as a fallback when needed.

APPEARANCE IN PROFILE
Choose dark or light mode from Profile settings. Your appearance preference is saved on your device.

Your existing timesheets, breaks, pay tools, workplaces, statements and hour restrictions remain available.
