Keep your working day organized with KaamKoRecord. Track your shifts, review your hours and search your own item lists in one app.

TRACK YOUR WORK
Clock in and out, record breaks and review your shifts in a list or calendar. Save workplaces, organize your work records and track hours against a weekly or fortnightly limit.

REVIEW YOUR HOURS AND PAY
Use your timesheets, pay tools and statements to keep track of your work. Save workplace details such as hourly rates and pay cycles for easier reference.

SEARCH YOUR OWN ITEMS
Import a CSV file and choose the columns you want to use for item titles, descriptions, codes and search. Use PLU codes, grocery lists or your own catalogue structure.

KEEP CATALOGUES SEPARATE
Save named catalogues such as Meat or Groceries. Choose which list to search and update one catalogue without replacing the others. Your item catalogues belong to your signed-in account.

STAY CONNECTED
Browse the notice board, share stories and find updates in Alerts. An unread badge helps you see what is waiting, and app notifications can take you to the relevant screen.

MAKE IT YOURS
Move between Home, Items, Work, Alerts and Profile. Choose light or dark mode in Profile and keep your appearance preference on your device.

An account and an internet connection are required to use KaamKoRecord.
