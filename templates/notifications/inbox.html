That vocabulary belongs to the board (see noticeboard/_notice.html) and is borrowed here on purpose — a person is the same colour everywhere in MyWork, and the thing that tells you about a notice should look related to it. Opening this page is what marks it read. The rows that were unread when you arrived still show as unread, because that is what you came to see; it is the next visit that comes back quiet.
