Being paid restarts the counting. The big figure is the hours worked since the money arrived — back to zero on the day it does — while the hours before it stay in the bar as a paler length and stay in the period's total. A cap of 48 hours a fortnight is 48 whether or not somebody paid you halfway through it, so the reset is a place to count from and never a second allowance. {% endcomment %}
