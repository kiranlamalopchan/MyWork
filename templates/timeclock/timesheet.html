{% comment %} ---- Totals -------------------------------------------------------------- As many as you are paid over, and no more. Paid weekly, a fortnight's hours answer no question you have; paid monthly, all three are steps on the way to the figure that turns up. Paid whenever your employer gets round to it, neither a week nor a fortnight means anything and the only figure worth showing is what has piled up since the money last arrived — the one that goes back to zero the day it arrives again. The view decides which — see CYCLE_PERIODS and _since_paid — and the grid widens to fit however many that leaves. {% endcomment %}
