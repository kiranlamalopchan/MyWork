One card per workplace and never one total across them: two jobs pay on two different days, and a single figure would be cleared by whichever of them paid first, taking the other's hours with it. Each card is the figure, how it breaks down, and one form under it: what the payment covered — a pay run, everything up to now, or work up to a day you name — and the button. There used to be a button on the card and a second page with three more ways of drawing the same line; this is that line, drawn in one place. Marking a payment received changes nothing on your timesheet. It draws a line at that moment, and the hours before it stop being counted as owed — which is why the line can be rubbed out again and the shifts behind it are untouched either way. {% endcomment %} {% block content %} {% icon "back" %} More
