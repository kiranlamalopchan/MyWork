Privacy Policy

Effective {{ effective|date:"j F Y" }} · applies to the KaamKoRecord website and the KaamKoRecord app for iPhone and Android.

In short

  • KaamKoRecord keeps only what it needs to run your timesheet and pay, your item catalogues, the notice board and notifications.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to track you. There is no analytics or advertising software in the app or on the site.
  • Your shifts, pay, tax settings, catalogues and contact details are private to you. Posts and stories are shown only to the audience you choose.
  • Other members can see your name, photo and whether you are using KaamKoRecord right now — nothing more unless you post it.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, at any time from the app or the site.

1. Who is responsible

KaamKoRecord is a personal work-tracking service operated by its developer{% if contact_email %}, who can be reached at {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}. This policy explains what information KaamKoRecord collects when you use the website or the app, why it is collected, who can see it, and what control you have over it. KaamKoRecord aims to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

2. What we collect

Almost everything KaamKoRecord holds is information you enter yourself. The only things it records on its own are when you were last active and the details listed under "Activity status" and "Device" below. It does not buy, infer or collect data about you from anywhere else.

3. On your device

  • Camera, microphone and photos: asked for only when you choose to take or attach a photo or video, and used only for that.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: if you turn on biometric sign-in, the check happens entirely on your device. KaamKoRecord never receives or stores any biometric information — only a yes or no from your device.
  • Sign-in token and settings: the app keeps its sign-in token in your device's secure storage, and small settings such as light or dark mode on the device itself.
  • Not used: the app does not read your contacts, location, calendar or other files.

4. How we use it

Your information is used solely to provide KaamKoRecord to you: keeping your timesheet, working out pay and tax estimates, searching your catalogues, making the PDFs you ask for (statements and picking lists), showing the notice board and stories, delivering notifications you have turned on, keeping the community safe, and answering support requests you send.

Tax figures are estimates worked out on our server from the published ATO withholding formulas. They are not sent to the ATO, your employer or anyone else.

It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold, rented or given to third parties for their own purposes.

5. Who can see it

  • Only you: your workplaces, shifts, breaks, payments, pay and tax settings, timesheet, statements, catalogues, and the contact details on your profile.
  • The audience you choose: each notice, comment or story is shown to everyone signed in, to your friends, or to you alone — whichever you picked when posting — along with your name and photo.
  • The owner of a story: when you view someone's story, they can see that you viewed it.
  • Other signed-in members: your username, display name, photo and activity status (the green dot), so they can find you and send friend requests.
  • Moderators: when something is reported, the developer reviews the report and the reported content.
  • Nobody else. Public holidays, the daily quote and joke are shared reference content and are not about you.

You can block anyone, after which neither of you sees the other's posts, and report any post or person; reports are reviewed within 24 hours. The community rules say what is not allowed.

6. Services that carry your information

KaamKoRecord contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. A few services are needed to run it, and each receives only what it needs:

  • Hosting: the website, the app's data and your uploads are stored on a server run by PythonAnywhere.
  • Password recovery emails: when email recovery is enabled, Brevo receives your saved email address, username and one-use reset link to deliver the message. It does not receive your password or work records.
  • App notifications: notifications to the app travel through Expo's push service and then Apple's or Google's, which receive the notification's text and your device token so they can deliver it.
  • Website notifications: notifications to a browser travel through that browser's own push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla), encrypted so that service cannot read them.
  • App stores: Apple and Google handle downloading and updating the app under their own privacy policies.

7. Where it is stored, and for how long

Your information is stored on the server that hosts the KaamKoRecord website and is kept for as long as your account exists, with these exceptions:

  • Stories stop showing 24 hours after they are posted and are then deleted, with their photo or video.
  • Notifications are deleted 30 days after they are sent, read or not.
  • Imported spreadsheets are discarded after one hour, and photos you ask us to read are not kept at all.

Deleting your account removes your account, profile, photo, work and pay records, catalogues, posts, comments, reactions, stories, friendships, blocks, reports you made, support requests and device tokens at once.

Two things remain after deletion. Where you had commented on or reacted to another member's post, that member keeps the notification they were sent at the time, with your name removed from it, until it reaches 30 days old like any other. And a report someone else made about you stays on record for moderation, with your account no longer attached to it.

8. Security

All traffic between the app or your browser and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to your account from the app is by a private token held in your device's secure storage, which you can revoke at any time by signing out. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but KaamKoRecord uses recognised, current practice to protect what it holds.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything KaamKoRecord holds about you is visible and editable in the app and on the site.
  • Take a copy: download a statement of your hours, pay and payments as a PDF, for any dates, from your profile.
  • Choose your audience: pick who sees each post or story when you share it, and delete anything you have posted at any time.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your device's settings at any time; the device token is then no longer used.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account; on the site, use the delete page in Settings. Both ask for your password and then remove everything described in section 7. Deleting your account is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Ask a question or make a complaint: use the contact details in section 12. If you are in Australia and not satisfied with the answer, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

10. Children

KaamKoRecord is intended for people who work and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date. Changes that materially affect how your information is used will be announced on the notice board.
