Privacy Policy

Effective {{ effective|date:"j F Y" }} · applies to the MeroKaam website and the MeroKaam app for iPhone and Android.

In short

  • MeroKaam keeps only what it needs to run your timesheet, the notice board and notifications.

1. Who is responsible

MeroKaam is a personal work-tracking service operated by its developer{% if contact_email %}, who can be reached at {{ contact_email }}{% endif %}. This policy explains what information MeroKaam collects when you use the website or the app, why it is collected, who can see it, and what control you have over it.

2. What we collect

Everything MeroKaam holds is information you enter yourself. It does not buy, infer or collect data from anywhere else.

InformationWhat it isWhy
Account Username, password and the date you joined. The password is stored as a salted hash and can't be read back. To sign you in and keep your account yours.
Profile optional Display name, email address, phone number, one line of address and a photo. Shown on your profile and, if you post, beside your posts.
Work The workplaces you add with their pay settings, and every shift, break and payment you record. To keep your timesheet and work out your pay.
Posts Notices, comments, reactions and stories, together with the audience you chose for each. To show them to the people you chose.
Friends Who you are friends with, and friend requests sent or received. To work out who your "friends only" posts reach.
Device optional A push-notification token and the device's name, only if you turn notifications on. To deliver notifications to that device.
Time zone The time zone your device reports. So shifts and posts show in your local time.

The app asks for the camera, microphone or photo library only when you choose to take or attach a photo or video. It does not read your contacts, location or files.

3. How we use it

Your information is used solely to provide MeroKaam to you: keeping your timesheet, showing the notice board, delivering notifications you have turned on, and answering support requests you send. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold, rented or given to third parties for their own purposes.

MeroKaam contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. The only third-party service involved is the push-notification delivery service of your device's platform (Apple or Google), which receives the notification's text so it can be delivered.

4. Who can see it

  • Only you: your workplaces, shifts, breaks, payments, timesheet and the contact details on your profile.
  • The audience you choose: each notice, comment or story is shown to everyone signed in, to your friends, or to you alone — whichever you picked when posting — along with your display name and photo.
  • Other signed-in members: your username, display name and photo, so they can find you and send friend requests.
  • Nobody else. Public holidays and PLU codes are shared reference data and are not about you.

You can block anyone, after which neither of you sees the other's posts, and report any post or person; reports are reviewed within 24 hours. The community rules say what is not allowed.

5. Where it is stored, and for how long

Your information is stored on the server that hosts the MeroKaam website and is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your account, profile, work records, posts, reactions, stories, friendships and device tokens at once.

One thing remains after deletion: where you had commented on or reacted to another member's post, that member keeps the notification they were sent at the time, with your name removed from it.

6. Security

All traffic between the app or your browser and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to your account from the app is by a private token held in your device's secure storage, which you can revoke at any time by signing out. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but MeroKaam uses recognised, current practice to protect what it holds.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything MeroKaam holds about you is visible and editable in the app and on the site.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your device's settings at any time; the device token is then no longer used.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open Profile → Delete account; on the site, use your profile's delete page. Both ask for your password and then remove everything described in section 5. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Ask a question or make a request: use the contact details in section 10.

8. Children

MeroKaam is intended for people who work and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date. Changes that materially affect how your information is used will be announced on the notice board.
