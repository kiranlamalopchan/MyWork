{% load humanize avatars ui %} {% comment %} One notice: who wrote it, what they wrote, what the room made of it, and the thread underneath. Rendered by _board.html both above and below the fold, so a notice looks and behaves the same whichever side of it it lands on. Top to bottom it is laid out the way a post on a social app is: the author line, the words, the tally of faces and comments, one row of actions, then the thread. The card stretches everything to its own width (see .notice in §25), so the row of actions is one row and the reply box reaches the edge the card does. Takes: notice (decorated by views.decorate), next_url, emoji_choices. The include of _board.html also supplies `form`, which the editor on your own notice reads. {% endcomment %} {% comment %} A notice arrives the way a notification does: washed in the colour of whoever sent it, lifted off the page, and with a dot on it while it is still new. The colour comes from the name itself, so a person is the same colour on every device with nothing assigned or stored. Your own are the exception and stay plain. A notification is something somebody sent you; what you posted yourself is not news to you, so it gets no tint, no dot and a quiet brand rail down the edge instead. {% endcomment %}
