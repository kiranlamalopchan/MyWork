KaamKoRecord / proposed design

Your work and your items, easier to find.

A mobile layout organised around everyday tasks. This interactive concept uses sample data; it does not change your account or the working app.

  1. 01 · Give Home a practical purposeSearch items, start a shift, and review your week first. Stories, notices, holidays, and daily extras sit below.
  2. 02 · Make catalogues easy to manageSearch and My catalogues share one Items tab. The selected source stays visible above the search box.
  3. 03 · Bring work tools togetherClock, Timesheets, Pay, Workplaces, and hour restrictions live in Work. Keep limits and reset cycles visible beside your clock.
  4. 04 · Make uploads predictableChoose file → choose headings → review and save. Create a new catalogue by default.
  5. 05 · Give account settings a clear homeProfile holds friends, notification preferences, appearance, privacy, and help. The bell opens your inbox.

Try the bottom tabs, switch Meat to Groceries, search “001”, or preview the upload steps.
